Jev for Excel

Classify, score and check text with AI, right in your formulas

Jev reads the text in your cells and returns an answer you can sort, filter and chart: which category fits, where the text sits on a scale, or how likely a statement is to be true. Fill a formula down a column to process every row.

  • =JEV.CHOICE(A2, "billing", "technical", "other")technical
  • =JEV.SCORE(A2, "Calm", "Annoyed", "Angry")1.43
  • =JEV.NOUL(A2, "Asks for a refund")0.93

=JEV.ASK("choice", A2, "billing", "technical", "other")
=JEV.ASK("score", A2, "Calm", "Frustrated", "Angry")
=JEV.ASK("noul", A2, "Asks for a refund")

=JEV.CHOICE(A2, "?Which team?", $F$2:$F$6)
=JEV.SCORE(A2, $H$2:$H$4)
=JEV.NOUL(A2, "Mentions a deadline")
